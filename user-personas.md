# User Personas

## Persona 1: International Food Distributor

### Background

Food distributor looking for premium suppliers with high quality standards and certifications.

### Goals

- Find reliable suppliers
- Verify certifications
- Request commercial information
- Build long-term partnerships

### Pain Points

- Difficult website navigation
- Lack of trust indicators
- Limited product information

### Needs

- Easy access to certifications
- Product details
- Contact information

## Persona 2: Wholesale Buyer

### Background

Business customer searching for premium grass-fed beef products.

### Goals

- Compare suppliers
- Evaluate product quality
- Contact sales team

### Pain Points

- Excessive content
- Difficulty finding key information

### Needs

- Clear navigation
- Strong product showcase
- Easy communication channels
